Who I am

I'm Aníbal. For twenty years I've turned ideas into digital products across AI, IoT, e-commerce and cloud, in SaaS, B2B and B2C.

I started as the designer who shapes the experience. Over time I became the person who also decides what gets built, in what order and why: as a founder for ten years, then as Product Owner and Associate Product Manager on AI products.

I start from evidence, such as interviews, observation and the documents a company already has, and turn it into something a team can commit to: a service blueprint, a backlog, a prototype. I use AI tools to prototype in code and to move faster through research and specs.

Away from product, I follow tech and macro trends, physics and cosmology, play board games with friends, and enjoy the rewarding work of being a parent.
