About me

Senior Product Designer and Product Owner, based in Porto and working remotely.

I design and run digital products, mostly for businesses: AI tools, IoT platforms, SaaS and e-commerce. For twenty years I've worked on both sides of the table, as the designer who shapes the experience and as the product owner or manager who decides what gets built, in what order and why.

I'm at my best where a product meets messy reality: a library backlog of 50,000 books, a building with thousands of lighting devices, a bank's very first login. At the National Library of the Netherlands I owned the backlog of an AI cataloguing tool and designed its review screens, and cataloguing time per book fell by 40 to 50%.

I start from evidence (interviews, observation, and the documents a company already has) and turn it into something a team can commit to: a service blueprint, a backlog, a prototype. I set targets before we build, and I use AI tools to prototype in code and to move faster through research and specs.

How I work

  • I make AI decisions visible.Field-level match scores in KB RetroTool, "Why this answer" in Sancus: people trust what they can check.
  • I set the targets before we build.Every phase of the KB RetroTool roadmap had its own success metrics before work started.
  • I design systems, not only screens.A design system adopted across Tridonic's apps (TDS), a service blueprint that reconciled seven sources at Feedzai.
  • I treat scope as a product decision.Explicit trade-offs in Maya, fixed packs at WebLicious, MVP lines drawn at Sancus.
